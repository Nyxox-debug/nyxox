I'm Victor Chinekezi, a computer science student and software engineer focused on backend systems, systems programming, AI infrastructure, and developer tooling.

I enjoy learning how software works beneath its abstractions. That curiosity has led me to build programming languages, automatic differentiation engines, rendering systems, repository analysis tools, and AI-powered infrastructure.

Some of the projects I'm most proud of include:

- [OKIOS](/posts/projects/artificial-life-simulation/), a 3D artificial life simulation where neural-network-driven creatures evolve survival behaviour.
- [Synap](/posts/projects/deep-learning-framework-scratch/), a minimal deep-learning framework with a C++ tensor and autograd engine.
- [Bat](/posts/projects/programming-language-go/), an interpreted programming language built in Go.
- [Repo Scan](/posts/projects/github-analyzer-go-python/), a static-analysis tool for exploring GitHub repositories.

I use this site to document what I build, explain the ideas behind my projects, and share what I learn while becoming a better engineer.

You can explore my [projects](/posts/projects/), view my [portfolio](/portfolio/), or find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/Nyxox-debug).
